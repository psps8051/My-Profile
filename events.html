सूर्यनमस्कार दिन
Suryanamaskar Day

A vibrant annual celebration dedicated to Suryanamaskar — the complete salutation to the sun. Members of all batches gather at sunrise to perform rounds in unison, honouring the sun's life-giving energy and the strength it builds within each of us. A high-energy, joyful morning that fills everyone with positivity for the day ahead.

योगदिन
International Yoga Day · 21 June

Every year on 21st June, we celebrate International Yoga Day with great enthusiasm — our largest community event. We step beyond the studio walls and take yoga to open public spaces, inviting not just our members but the entire neighbourhood to experience yoga's transformative power. Free for all, full of joy.

गुरुपौर्णिमा
Guru Purnima — Honouring the Teacher

Gurupurnima is the most sacred and heartfelt celebration at our centre. On this full-moon day, we pause to express our deepest gratitude to our Guru — Yoga Guru Shri BB Sir — whose guidance has shaped everything we do. It is a day of reverence, reflection, and the beautiful eternal bond between teacher and student.

गरबा · भोंडला
Garba & Bhondla — Festive Celebrations

Our studio comes alive with colour, music, and joyful energy during the festive season! We celebrate the traditional Maharashtrian festival of Bhondla and the vibrant Garba-Dandiya evenings — bringing all members together for an evening of dance, song, and togetherness. Because true wellness includes joy, laughter, and celebration.

सहल · स्नेहमेळावा
Annual Outing & Get-Together

Every year, each batch goes on a happiness trip — our beloved Sahal and Snehamelava. This annual outing is a time to step away from routine, breathe in nature, deepen friendships, and celebrate the bonds formed through shared practice on the mat. It is not just a trip — it is a reunion of hearts. A cherished tradition that members look forward to all year long.
