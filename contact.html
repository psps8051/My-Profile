Quick Answers

Common questions.

Do I need to bring my own mat?

No — we provide high-quality mats, blocks, belts, and bolsters for all students. You're welcome to bring your own if you prefer.

Can I try a class before committing?

Absolutely. First-time students get a complimentary drop-in class. Just arrive 10 minutes early and introduce yourself at reception.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, stretchy clothing you can move freely in. Yoga is practiced barefoot — no shoes on the studio floor please.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes — we stream selected classes live on Zoom and have a growing library of recorded classes. Ask us about our digital membership.
