Our Story

From 4 members to
a thriving community of 125.

Our journey began under the guidance of Yoga Guru Shri BB Sir on 1st November 2011. In these 10+ glorious years, our member strength has grown from just 4 to 125 who practice yoga every single day. Our classes have benefited hundreds of fitness enthusiasts, senior citizens, and pregnant women till date.

Our aim is to spread awareness of yoga in daily life — not just for physical and mental health, but for spiritual and social wellbeing too. With that vision, we conduct four different batches in mornings and evenings so members can find time for yoga within their busy schedules.

Specialised Batches

Dedicated regular batches for Senior Citizens and Pregnant Women cater to their specific needs. Our Meditation batch helps members achieve mind relaxation and overcome daily stress. Yoga Therapy sessions are also arranged to address individual health concerns.

👩
Women's Health & Wellness

Special attention is given to improving women's health, fitness, and ability to handle hormonal changes. Women ranging from 18 to 75 years of age are benefiting from yoga practices through regular exercise at our centre.

🎉
Community & Social Health

We celebrate together throughout the year — Yoga Day, Suryanamaskar Day, Women's Day, Gurupurnima, Dandiya and Bhondla bring all members closer. We also arrange a happiness trip every year for all batches.

🤲
Service to Society

We believe in giving back. During the COVID pandemic we conducted a free 15-day yoga camp for prevention and recovery, receiving an overwhelming response and positive feedback from the community.

💻
Online & Offline — Your Choice

The pandemic encouraged us to take our classes online for everyone's safety. Today we conduct both Online as well as Offline classes, giving our members the flexibility to practice from wherever they are.

What We Stand For

Our guiding principles.

🌱
Ahimsa — Non-Harm

We teach yoga with deep respect for every body. No competition, no comparison — only compassionate guidance at your own pace.

🔍
Svadhyaya — Self-Study

Every session is an invitation to know yourself more deeply. We create conditions for genuine self-inquiry, both on and off the mat.

🌊
Santosha — Contentment

We believe in celebrating where you are right now. Progress in yoga is not linear — and that is the most beautiful thing about it.

🤝
Sangha — Community

Yoga is better together. Our studio is a living, breathing community — we gather for workshops, festivals, and everyday practice.

📚
Jnana — Knowledge

Our teachers are students first. Continuous training, annual retreats, and ongoing study keep our teaching deep, current, and alive.

🌍
Seva — Service

We run monthly donation-based classes and partner with Pune NGOs to make yoga accessible to those who need it most.
